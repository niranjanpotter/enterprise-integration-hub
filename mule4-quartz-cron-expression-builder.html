5-Field Linux Cron vs 6-Field Mule 4 Quartz

Linux cron has 5 fields starting with Minutes. Mule 4, Spring Boot, and Quartz have 6 fields starting with Seconds. Our builder translates both and gives you the exact `<scheduling-strategy><cron .../></scheduling-strategy>` snippet.

Quick Questions Developers Ask Me

Q: Why does Mule 4 reject standard 5-field cron expressions?

Because Mule 4 uses the Quartz engine, which requires 6 fields (starting with Seconds) and a '?' wildcard on Day-of-Month or Day-of-Week.
