5-Field Unix vs 6-Field Quartz (Mule 4 / Spring)

Standard Unix cron starts with Minutes (5 fields), whereas Mule 4 and Quartz Scheduler start with Seconds (6 fields) and require `?` in either Day-of-Month or Day-of-Week. Our builder detects the format automatically and outputs ready-to-paste `<scheduling-strategy><cron .../></scheduling-strategy>` XML.

Frequently Asked Architect Questions

Q: Why does Mule 4 Scheduler fail on a standard 5-field Linux cron?

Mule 4 uses Quartz Cron (6 fields starting with Seconds) and requires a '?' wildcard in either Day-of-Month or Day-of-Week.
