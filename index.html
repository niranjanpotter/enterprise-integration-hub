01 · Honest 2026 Platform Comparison

Cross-Platform Radar - I read the official docs so you don't have to

Every week someone asks me - MuleSoft or Workato? Salesforce or SAP? Honestly, there is no best platform. There is only the right platform for YOUR use case. I went through the 2026 official docs of all 4 platforms and put the real differences here. No marketing fluff. No sales talk.

MuleSoft

Deep Control & Hybrid

Best when you need deep, complex integrations and full control. Slow to start, but rock solid once it's running. Scoped OAuth. JWT + Agent Fabric (agent-network.yaml), Omni Gateway (the 2026 evolution of Flex Gateway), and CloudHub 2.0 Kubernetes private spaces when you can't afford a single dropped transaction.

Workato

Fast Business Speed

Best when your business team wants to move fast without waiting for IT. Super quick, but gets messy on very complex flows. Verified User Access (VUA) runs every MCP tool call under the end user's own OAuth login instead of a dangerous master token, plus Slack and Teams human-in-the-loop approvals.

Salesforce Agentforce

CRM-Native AI

Best if you already live inside Salesforce. Your agents can act on your data directly. Just don't give them god-mode access. Topics + Actions pair with External Services (OpenAPI), Data Cloud Zero-Copy, and the gRPC Pub/Sub API with pull flow control so event bursts don't choke your subscribers.

SAP

S/4HANA Clean Core

Best if your core is SAP. Nothing beats it for SAP-to-SAP. Outside SAP, you'll need help. Clean Core on SAP BTP keeps custom logic out of S/4HANA ABAP using OData v4, SAP Graph, and Advanced Event Mesh so ERP upgrades don't break your weekend.
