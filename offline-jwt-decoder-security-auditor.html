What It Checks Automatically

As soon as you paste your token, it decodes the Base64URL header and payload right inside your browser tab. It checks if the token is expired, warns you if `alg` is set to `none`, flags tokens that live longer than 24 hours, and tells you if `iss` or `aud` claims are missing.

We Never Store It. Promise.

There is no backend database, no analytics tracking your input, and no network call when you paste a token. You can even turn off your Wi-Fi after loading the page and it still works.

Quick Questions Developers Ask Me

Q: Does my JWT token ever leave my laptop?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser's JavaScript engine with zero network calls.
